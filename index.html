How we work

Every project runs the same loop of four steps. The fourth is the one most studios skip, and it is where the best work happens.

  1. Listen

    Two weeks with your customers, your data and your front line before we draw anything at all.

  2. Make

    Small mixed teams ship real, testable things early: in days, not quarters, and in front of real people.

  3. Measure

    We agree on what better looks like before we start, then report it honestly, good news or not.

  4. Again

    We take what we learned and go round once more. It is not the end of the project. It is the job.

Fair questions

What size of company do you work with?

Mostly companies between fifty and five thousand people, plus a few public bodies. Size matters less than whether the people who decide are in the room.

How does a project usually start?

With a two-week listening sprint: interviews, data and a day on your front line. You get a short written point of view and a plan before any big commitment.

Do you work alongside in-house teams?

Almost always. We pair with your designers, engineers and marketers, and we hand over every system we build so you are never stuck with us. Most partners keep us around anyway.

How do you price the work?

Fixed fees for defined phases, then a monthly team for ongoing work. We are open about our rates on the first call.

Where are you based?

Lisbon and Brooklyn, with people working remotely across Europe and the US east coast. We travel for workshops and launches.
